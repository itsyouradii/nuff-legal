## What Nuff is

Nuff helps you spend less time on feeds, video apps and games. It watches how long they stay open and steps in with a pause, a question or a wait. It is a self-help tool and not medical or psychological care. If your phone use is hurting your health, please talk to a professional.

## Who can use it

You need to be 18 or older to use Nuff.

## The choices you make

You decide which apps Nuff watches and how strict it is. Some choices hold on purpose: once you start a guard, a blocking schedule or a chosen time, Nuff gives no free passes until it ends. You can always leave an app at once, and you can end a guard or a schedule early from inside Nuff after a short wait.

Nuff watches feeds, video apps and games only. Even so, it may slow you down in an app at a moment you need it, so do not depend on a watched app in an emergency.

You can switch off any of Nuff's permissions in Android's settings at any time. Nuff then stops doing what that permission was for.

## Price

Nuff has a free plan and a paid plan, Nuff+. During the beta nothing is sold and everything is on.

Once Nuff+ is on sale, your first week has everything, with nothing to pay. After that the free plan keeps Nuff on one app of your choice, and Nuff+ covers every app and adds the notification filter. A guard or a blocking schedule works on any app on either plan. You see the price, and what you get, before you pay.

Nuff+ is bought through Google Play: as a subscription for a month or a year, or once for good. A subscription renews at the price shown until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Google Play, and Nuff+ then runs to the end of the time you paid for. If a plan starts with free days, you are charged when they end unless you cancel before. Payments, renewals and refunds are handled by Google Play under its terms.

If a subscription ends, Nuff goes back to the free plan. Nothing you set up is deleted.
